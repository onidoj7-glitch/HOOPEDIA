Positions and Skills

Traditional basketball positions describe common roles on a team. Modern players often share responsibilities, and position names do not define every player's abilities.

On this page

  1. Five traditional positions
  2. Skills for every player

Five traditional positions

Common roles on a basketball team
PositionCommon responsibilities
Point guardOften organizes the offense, handles the ball, and helps teammates find scoring opportunities.
Shooting guardOften contributes perimeter shooting, off-ball movement, and ball handling.
Small forwardOften fills a versatile role, contributing to scoring, defense, and rebounding.
Power forwardOften plays near the basket as well as in space, helping with screens, defense, and rebounding.
CenterOften plays close to the basket, protecting the rim and contesting rebounds and inside shots.

These are traditional descriptions, not strict rules. Teams may use lineups in which several players handle the ball, shoot from the perimeter, or defend multiple positions.

Skills for every player

  • Dribbling: control the ball while moving and respond to defensive pressure.
  • Passing: deliver the ball accurately and make decisions that help the team.
  • Shooting: use sound technique and choose an appropriate attempt.
  • Rebounding: position for and secure the ball after a missed shot.
  • Defense: use footwork, awareness, and communication to contest opponents legally.
  • Teamwork: communicate, move without the ball, and make decisions for the benefit of the team.

Read more about these fundamentals on Plays and Techniques.
