Basketball History

Basketball began as an indoor team game in the United States in the late nineteenth century and developed into a sport played around the world.

On this page

  1. Invention of the game
  2. Development and spread
  3. The modern game

Invention of the game

Basketball was invented in 1891 by James Naismith, a physical education instructor at the International YMCA Training School in Springfield, Massachusetts. He was asked to create an indoor activity for students during the winter. The first game used a soccer ball and peach baskets as goals, and its original rules emphasized passing and a high target.

Development and spread

The game spread through schools and YMCA programs. As it grew, equipment and rules changed: purpose-built hoops and backboards replaced the original baskets, and dribbling became part of play. Organized competitions and professional teams developed during the twentieth century.

Basketball became an Olympic medal sport for men in 1936 and for women in 1976. International competition helped the game gain a wider audience and encouraged play across many countries.

The modern game

Today basketball is played at recreational, school, collegiate, professional, and international levels. Rules and playing styles differ across competitions, while the basic aim—scoring in the opponent's basket and defending one's own—remains recognizable.
